Can you spot the AI?

AI image generators are getting really good. Adults get fooled all the time. Let's practice the small "tells" that give a fake away — and learn to know when you actually know.

You'll see a series of scenes. For each one, pick Real or AI-generated, and set how confident you are. You'll score more when you're confident and right — and lose more when you're confident and wrong.

Scoring & calibration

Each round: points = ±confidence. If you're 90% confident and right, +90. If 90% confident and wrong, −90. Set 50% if you're just guessing — that move barely changes your score. Over time, your calibration % shows how well your confidence matches reality.
