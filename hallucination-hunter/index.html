The Game

AI models sometimes make up fake citations, wrong dates, invented people, or made-up quotes — and say them with total confidence. Your job: read each AI-style passage, click the sentences (or selected phrases) that look suspicious, tag what kind of mistake you think it is, then submit to see the ground truth.

Tip: Be skeptical of oddly-specific numbers, journal names you've never heard of, and dramatic quotes. But don't over-call — flagging real facts costs you points.
