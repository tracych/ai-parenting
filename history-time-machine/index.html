How to use the Time Machine

The big idea: History isn't a single line. While Rome built the Colosseum, the Maya were inventing rubber, and Tang China was perfecting block printing. Everything was happening at once, everywhere.

🔀 Sort it! — App scrambles event cards from different civilizations. You drag them into the right order.

🌍 Meanwhile, in… — Pick an event. The app reveals three other things happening on Earth at the same moment. No score — just wow.

🎯 Year Explorer — Type a year. See a snapshot per civilization. Try 1000 CE. Try 500 BCE. Try 1492.
