About Joke Lab

A workshop for kids and grown-ups to invent jokes together. Pick a shape, pick a topic, edit a seed, and let the deterministic AI rater hand out 1–5 stars with a tiny piece of feedback.

How the rater works

No model, no internet. The rater scores three things:

  • Surprise — how many words appear in the punchline that didn't appear in the setup? A bigger twist scores higher.
  • Punch — short punchlines score higher. (Brevity is the soul of funny.)
  • Length-fit — the setup should be roughly 1.5–4× the length of the punchline. Too-balanced or too-lopsided shapes lose a point.

Same joke text → same score, every time. Edit the joke and watch the stars climb.

Privacy

Everything lives in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Ever.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Read the seed out loud first. Kids hear rhythm better than they see it.
  • Don't fix the joke for them. Suggest moves: "what if it ended on a food word?"
  • Groan-worthy is great. The worse the pun, the bigger the laugh later.
  • Use Present mode at dinner. Real audiences make kids edit better than any rater.
