About Backyard Scavenger

A pocket-sized excuse to get outside. Pick a season, get a list of stuff to look for, and snap a photo when you find it. Each item has a ? button — a quick kid-friendly hint about how to recognize it.

Family team mode: add a couple of players and assign items to a specific kid (or leave them as "anyone").

Print mode: hit the 🖨️ Print button on the hunt card for a paper version — useful for car trips, classrooms, or just unplugging.

Photos stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded — everything lives in your browser's local storage.

Works offline. No login, no GPS, no tracking. Made for muddy hands.
